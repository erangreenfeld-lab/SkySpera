Energy & Renewables

Transmission-line and pipeline corridor monitoring

Grid and pipeline operators run assets across thousands of kilometres where vegetation encroachment, third-party digging, erosion and landslides threaten continuity — risks no inspection schedule catches in time. SkySpera watches the corridor centreline with 1 m visual and 2 m multispectral differenced against its own baseline, priced per kilometre, so an entire network is monitored on one schedule instead of paying per-pass tasking fees (the packaged Corridor Watching service is in development; the underlying imagery and change layers are available today); it pairs with firebreak-condition and leak layers on the same imagery.
