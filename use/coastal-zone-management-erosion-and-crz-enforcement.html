Coastal authorities must manage erosion and enforce coastal-regulation zones along shorelines that shift year to year — where illegal construction, deforestation and unpermitted jetties creep in. SkySpera's deep 1 m archive makes shoreline change, groyne works and coastal development directly measurable over years, as demonstrated across dozens of Indian coastal sites, supporting objective CZMP/CRZ monitoring and enforcement.
