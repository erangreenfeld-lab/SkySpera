Grassland Biomass

Which paddock to move stock to next — a 2 m relative biomass ranking in t/ha, auto-calibrated to geography, correct 90%+ of the time.

Graziers need a relative ranking of standing biomass across fields, refreshed often enough to act on. SkySpera computes a composite biomass index at 2 m from the full 10-band stack, auto-calibrated to local geography so it works without per-site tuning. Absolute accuracy is ±30–35%; relative accuracy ±15–20%, ranking fields correctly more than 90% of the time. Built for the next grazing decision rather than scientific carbon accounting.

Where it’s used

  • Next-paddock grazing decisions; rangeland/forage management.

Technical

  • Delivered GSD: 2 m, 10-band stack. Units: t/ha. Calibration: auto per geography.
  • Accuracy: absolute ±30–35%; relative ±15–20% (90%+ correct field ranking); archive since 2015.
