Illegal Construction Detection

Find unpermitted building directly — pixels that classify as built today but were not built some months ago — across areas up to 100,000 km².

Unpermitted construction is small, dispersed and constant; by the time a structure is reported it is already built. SkySpera runs a land-cover classifier on two dates and applies a three-stage filter to suppress false positives — asymmetric strictness (the new date must be strongly artificial while any prior built hint is ignored), spectral confirmation (a real NDBI/brightness rise, not an alignment artefact), and a minimum component size (trims noise while keeping genuine small buildings). Running on enhanced 5 m super-resolution, it resolves individual structures (a 10×10 m house is several pixels) across areas up to 100,000 km², on an annual cycle, with per-site report views over an OSM reference.

Where it’s used

  • Planning-authority enforcement; land-registry and tax verification.

Technical

  • Delivered GSD: 5 m super-resolution. Coverage: up to 100,000 km²; annual cycle.
  • Method: two-date land-cover classification + 3-stage filter (asymmetric strictness, spectral confirmation, min component size).
  • Output: georeferenced change layers + per-site before/after over OSM.
