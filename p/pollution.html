Worldwide Pollution (Sentinel-5P)

Objective, comparable air-quality intelligence — the trace gases that define air quality, worldwide, daily, as absolute readings and change vs last year.

Sentinel-5P measures the trace gases that define air quality from orbit, worldwide, every day: NO₂ (traffic/combustion), SO₂ (power/industry), CO, CH₄, HCHO and aerosols. SkySpera delivers this in two complementary views: the absolute reading for each gas, and the delta against the same period last year, so seasonal cycles cancel and genuine change stands out. A factory coming online, a traffic rebound, an industrial shutdown or a multi-year trend surfaces as a clear anomaly — global coverage, daily refresh, no ground sensor network.

Where it’s used

  • Regulatory and health-agency air-quality monitoring.
  • Industrial-emissions change detection (new/idled sources).

Technical

  • Gases: NO₂, SO₂, CO, CH₄, HCHO, aerosol index.
  • Source: Copernicus Sentinel-5P; refresh: daily worldwide.
  • Views: absolute concentration + like-for-like year-on-year delta.
